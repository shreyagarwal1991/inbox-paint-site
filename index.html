Inbox Paint

Color rules for Gmail. Point at an email, click Color emails like this…, and every similar email stands out: clients in blue, invoices in orange, unread in red.

Need help? Email support@enuf.ai. Tell us your Chrome version and which Gmail view you were using. Please don't send screenshots of private mail. The diagnostics in Settings are safe to share: they hold counts only.

What it does

  • Teach by example: hover an email, click the Inbox Paint button, pick what makes it special (sender, domain, a subject word, a label), and create the rule. The preview counts the matches first.
  • Three looks: Accent (a thin colored edge), Soft (a light tint) and Strong (a full color with readable text).
  • Every colored row can tell you why: the rule that colored it, with Edit, Turn off, or make an exception for this sender or conversation.
  • Recipes for VIP senders, work domains, money, travel, newsletters and unread mail.
  • Gmail keeps working exactly as before: opening, selecting, starring and hover actions. Keys you type in the Inbox Paint panel never trigger Gmail shortcuts.
  • No sign-in, no Gmail API, no server. Everything runs in your browser, and your mailbox is never changed.

Common questions

Colors are not showing.
Open Inbox Paint from the toolbar. The status panel says whether it is working. If you installed it while Gmail was open, reload that Gmail tab once.
Some emails are not colored.
Rules run top to bottom and the first match wins. The rule editor shows how many visible conversations match, and how many a higher rule takes instead, with a one-click “Put this rule above”.
It says “Gmail layout not recognized”.
Google changed Gmail and Inbox Paint can't read the list safely, so it stopped coloring instead of guessing. Gmail looks as normal. An update will fix it.
I want Gmail's own look back.
Use the switch at the top of the Inbox Paint window. Gmail looks exactly as before.
Does it read or send my email?
It reads what the Gmail list already shows (sender, subject, preview, labels, unread and starred), only on mail.google.com, only to decide colors. It never opens emails and nothing leaves your browser. See the privacy policy.
Is it free?
Everything is unlocked during early access. If a paid plan comes later, early users keep what they have.
